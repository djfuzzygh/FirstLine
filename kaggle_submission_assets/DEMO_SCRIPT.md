# FirstLine Demo Script (2-3 Minutes)

**Role**: Narrator / CHW
**Setting**: A rural CHPS clinic in Ghana.

---

### [0:00 - 0:30] Introduction
*Visual: Open FirstLine app on a mobile device.*
"This is FirstLine. It’s an offline-first assistant for community health workers. Today, we’re seeing a 3-year-old patient who has been coughing and has a fever. In a busy clinic, it’s easy to miss subtle signs of pneumonia. FirstLine is here to help."

### [0:30 - 1:15] Intake & Adaptive Follow-up
*Visual: Navigating through IntakeScreen. Using voice input for symptoms.*
"I record the basics: Age 3, fever for 2 days. I use the voice intake powered by HAI-DEF's MedASR to capture the mother's description. Note how the app asks for patient consent immediately.
Once submitted, the system doesn't just calculate a score—it generates targeted follow-up questions. It asks: 'Is the child breathing faster than usual?' and 'Is there indrawing of the chest?' These are keyed to WHO IMCI guidelines."

### [1:15 - 2:00] Triage & Safety
*Visual: TriageScreen showing RED result.*
"The AI analyzes the inputs. Because the respiratory rate is recorded at 55—above the threshold for a 3-year-old—the system triggers a hard-coded Red-Flag. It recommends an urgent referral. 
Notice the uncertainty label. If the model were unsure, it would automatically upgrade the caution level. Most importantly, the final decision remains with me, the health worker."

### [2:00 - 2:45] Referral & Conclusion
*Visual: ReferralScreen with SOAP note.*
"Finally, FirstLine generates a complete SOAP note. This can be shared via text or a QR code to the district hospital. It ensures that when the patient arrives, the doctors have a clear, professional summary of what we observed in the field.
FirstLine is about grounded, responsible AI that respects the reality of the front line."

### [2:45 - 3:00] Closing
"Offline-first. Safety-driven. Human-centered. This is FirstLine."
