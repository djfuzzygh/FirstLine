## 🎯 Features Implemented

### **1. Voice Interface**
- ✅ Speech-to-Text (browser Web Speech API)
- ✅ Text-to-Speech (natural AI voice)
- ✅ Real-time transcription display
- ✅ Visual waveform animation

### **2. Full Triage Workflow**
- ✅ Collects patient age
- ✅ Collects patient sex
- ✅ Collects symptoms (natural language)
- ✅ Collects duration
- ✅ Asks WHO IMCI follow-up questions
- ✅ Performs AI triage
- ✅ Announces results via voice

### **3. Multi-Language Support**

- ✅ Twi (Akan)
- ✅ Ga
- ✅ Ewe

### **4. Professional UI**
- ✅ Phone-style interface
- ✅ Status indicators (calling, listening, thinking, speaking)
- ✅ Conversation log
- ✅ Live transcript
- ✅ Visual waveform
